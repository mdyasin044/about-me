01 — About

I'm now a Volunteer Research Assistant at EMIL Lab. Prior to this, I worked as a Senior Software Engineer at Samsung Research, and I graduated from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) with a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering.

My research focuses on the interface between clinical measurement and human-facing interpretation, with an emphasis on making physiological data usable and trustworthy for patients in real-world health settings. My work spans continuous glucose monitoring, diffusion-based medical imaging, time-series forecasting, and LLM-based patient education systems.

My long-term goal is to build health technologies that translate complex, noisy physiological signals into information patients can understand and act on, without sacrificing clinical rigor.
